# XR1 Studio v0.10 testing checklist

## Critical trim regression

1. Put a 10-second video clip on V1 at 00:00:10:00.
2. Drag the **left trim handle** two seconds to the right.
3. Confirm the clip now starts at 00:00:12:00 and still ends at 00:00:20:00.
4. Undo once and confirm the complete trim is restored in one step.
5. Repeat on the right edge and confirm the left edge stays fixed.

## Hit zones

- Drag the centre of a clip: the clip should move.
- Drag either edge handle: the source range should trim and the clip body must not move.
- Press Escape during either gesture: the clip should return to its original state.

## Linked A/V and groups

1. Use a V1 clip with linked A1 audio.
2. Group the video with an unrelated B-roll clip.
3. Trim the V1 video.
4. Confirm V1 and linked A1 trim together while the grouped B-roll is unchanged.
5. Disable sync lock on A1 and confirm the next trim affects picture only.

## Tools

- `A` or `V`: Selection tool.
- `T`: Trim tool; handles should be highlighted.
- `B`: Blade tool; a red frame guide should follow the pointer.
- Shift-click in Blade mode: every targeted unlocked track crossing that frame should split.
- `H`: Hand tool; drag the timeline without moving clips.
- `N`: Toggle snapping.
- Hold Alt while moving or trimming: snapping should be bypassed.

## Playback regression

- Place B-roll on V2 above a V1 video with A1 audio.
- Confirm V1 continues playing underneath and A1 remains audible.
- Lower V2 opacity or scale it down and confirm V1 is visible beneath it.
